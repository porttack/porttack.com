I'm heading into my first full year of teaching. Before the schedule fills up and the day-to-day takes over, here's what I actually believe about kids and teaching. I expect to edit it as the year tells me where I got it wrong.

<!--more-->

## Teaching Principles

* Youth represent humanity's future
* Character matters more than content
* Creativity matters more than consumption
* Growth matters more than overall accomplishment
* All students can succeed
* All living things — including students — deserve attention, respect, and inclusion
* High expectations produce high results

## Teaching Goals

* Students feel safe
* Help kids be the best version of themselves, in the classroom and out of it
* Students believe in themselves and in each other
* Students work on their own character, every week, not just when it comes up
* Creativity and mistakes are encouraged and rewarded
* Students find some intrinsic motivation weekly, not just a grade to chase
* Students reflect on their progress
* Students get better at actually listening
