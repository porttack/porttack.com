I tried the [One Word activity](/2025/09/22/one-word-wall.html) on my Rotary club, and it went well.

<!--more-->

My students do this one in the fall. They brainstorm words that describe who they are, cross them off until one is left, and that word goes on our classroom wall. I wondered whether it would work on adults who already know each other, in a meeting where the agenda usually wins.

The adult version is much shorter. Each person writes up to three lists of five words, narrows one list down to a single word, and writes it on a folded index card. Then we go around the room. Everyone says their word and, if they want, one sentence about why. I went first, since that sets the length everyone else matches.

I picked it partly because it fits Rotary's Four-Way Test. The first question is "Is it the truth?" The activity only works if the words are sincere, not performed for the room. It also does what the third question asks: it builds goodwill and better friendships. You learn something real about the person next to you that never comes up in club business.
