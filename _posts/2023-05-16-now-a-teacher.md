I've accepted a teaching position with SLVUSD for the 2023-24 school year, teaching [middle school and high school](/slv/) computer science.

<!--more-->

I wrote in March about [deciding to do this]({% post_url 2023-03-01-deciding-to-become-a-teacher %}). Now it has a start date.

I've been around this district a while already, as a parent, a scout leader, and Rotary youth chair, working on enrichment programs and parent groups. Standing at the front of the room is a different job, but it doesn't feel like showing up somewhere new. I don't know everyone yet. Plenty of teachers, parents, and professionals here have already gone out of their way to help me.

The decision took a while. It doesn't make financial sense, and I'd rather say that plainly than pretend otherwise. It does line up with what I actually care about.

New teachers have a hard first year, and I expect mine to be hard. What I want out of it is students who like computer science, and students who get better at character and at problems that don't come with an answer key.
