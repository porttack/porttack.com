8. Reporters asked computer security experts how video could come back from a camera that looked like it had no recording. Alex Stamos, a cybersecurity expert, told CBS News: "Internal storage uses a very lazy deletion mechanism, so the data wouldn't be available to users who didn't pay." He added: "The video for non-subscribers would be marked for deletion," and "the actual files might not be deleted for days."

9. Patrick Jackson, a former NSA data researcher, told CBS News: "There's kind of this old saying that data is never deleted, it's just renamed."

She also said that if law enforcement asked for footage before it was overwritten, it could still be retrieved.
