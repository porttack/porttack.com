1. During the 2026 Super Bowl, the doorbell camera company Ring ran an ad. In it, a family's dog runs away. Cameras all over the neighborhood help find it, and the dog comes home.

2. The feature is called Search Party. When you report a lost dog, Ring's AI looks through your neighbors' Ring cameras for a dog that matches. If a camera spots it, that neighbor can choose to share the video.

3. Search Party is turned on automatically for Ring cameras that save video online. Owners did not have to say yes. To turn it off, you have to find the setting yourself. One member of Congress said it was six steps deep in the app.

4. Many people were upset, from both political parties. Four days after the ad, Ring ended a deal with Flock Safety, a company that makes license plate cameras for police. Ring did not say the anger was the reason.

5. Ring says Search Party is only for pets. But Ring's founder, Jamie Siminoff, says Ring's real goal is to reduce crime. He has said its cameras could almost wipe out crime in some neighborhoods.
