Interactive · 49 provisions

The roll call

Every numbered provision in the bill, one square each, in order. Does it control somebody, grant a right against the system, or build a capability? Click any square to read it. Click a category button first, then a square, to sort it yourself.

How I sorted these

Control if the provision detects, restricts, punishes, mandates monitoring, or routes information about a person. Right if it protects someone from the system: appeals, notice, refusal, opt-out, limits on what officials may do. Capability if it makes a student or a teacher able to do something they could not do before.

Several are genuinely arguable. Section 2, provision 12 requires an oral defense to prove mastery, which sounds like capability until you notice it only triggers on suspicion. Section 3, provision 2 asks parents to learn AI literacy in order to monitor their children, which is both. I put the arguable ones where the provision's trigger points, not where its language points. If you sort them differently, the tally above is yours. That disagreement is the useful part.
