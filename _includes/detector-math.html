Why a better model does not fix this

The false positive rate applies to the honest majority, and there are a lot of them. The true positive rate applies to the small group who actually cheated. When the honest group is twenty times larger, a small error rate on that group produces more accusations than a large success rate on the other one. Vendors compete on the wrong number, because the number that matters is not accuracy. It is what fraction of the people you accuse did nothing wrong, and that depends on how many people cheated, which you do not know.

Set the false positive rate to zero and the problem disappears. No detector has ever achieved that, and the closest attempt, OpenAI's own classifier, was withdrawn in July 2023 for low accuracy six months after launch.
