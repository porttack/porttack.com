--- title: "Socratic Seminar Role Cards" permalink: /socratic-seminar-cards/ --- Socratic Seminar Role Cards

Socratic seminar role cards

One sheet makes four cards: two for each side. Print as many copies as you need and cut along the dashed lines. Hand a card to each student who will argue a side they did not pick. The sheet fills in from the lesson plan's link, or type your own below.
