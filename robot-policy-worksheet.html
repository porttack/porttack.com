One side of one sheet, for the day after the robot-teacher seminar. The rubric is printed on it so students know how it is graded.

A Rule About Robots in Classrooms

You are on the school board. Write one paragraph in complete sentences: the rule your district should adopt about humanoid robots in classrooms, and who decides. Say whether a robot may replace an aide, replace a teacher, or neither. Name one thing your rule protects and one thing it gives up. Use a detail from the reading, a video, or our discussion. It may help to think about who gets the robot and what student data it would hold.
