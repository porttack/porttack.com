## Your Mission

Think about yourself -- who you are, what matters to you, what makes you... YOU! Your goal is to choose **five words** that best describe you. These can be anything that feels true to who you are.

---

## Brainstorming Space

Before you pick your final five, brainstorm lots of possibilities! Think about:

- **Personality traits:** funny, curious, determined, kind...
- **Things you love:** music, sports, art, reading, gaming...
- **Your background:** culture, family traditions, places you're from...
- **Your goals or dreams:** what you want to become or achieve...
- **Your talents:** things you're good at or working on...

Write down any words that come to mind:

## Safe Space Reminder

Only share what you're comfortable sharing! If you want to include something personal that you don't want others to see, you can write a "code word" that only you understand. And remember -- don't suggest words for your classmates or comment on their choices.
