Socratic seminar handout

Two sides of one sheet. Print double-sided and flip on the long edge. The front is the entry ticket, including a standard question that asks for the best argument for the other side. The back is the observer organizer and the exit ticket.

With both sides filled in, the entry ticket asks students to circle one and rate how sure they are, 1 to 10, and the exit ticket has a Start and End line so you can tally who moved.
