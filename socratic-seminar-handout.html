Entry ticket

Fill this out after the reading, before the seminar starts.

1. Two questions the reading raises for you, ones you could actually argue about.

2. A starting side, and why. Changing your mind during the seminar is not losing. It is the point.

3. Two pieces of evidence from the reading, by paragraph number.

4. What is the best argument for the side you did not pick? Say it so well that someone who holds that view would agree you got it right.
